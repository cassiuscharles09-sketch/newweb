Student Life & Standards

Official School Uniform Reference

Visual guide and campus dress code policy for all registered students of Datamex College of St. Adeline.

Campus Dress Code & ID Policy

All students are strictly required to wear their complete official school uniform together with their valid DCSA Student ID & Official Lanyard upon entering any campus branch.

Female Uniform Specification

Official white uniform blouse with DCSA embroidered emblem, maroon necktie/scarf, pleated maroon skirt, white knee-high socks, and clean black closed shoes.

Male Uniform Specification

Official white uniform polo with DCSA necktie, tailored maroon trousers, dark socks, and polished black leather shoes. Undershirts must be plain white.

Uniform Availability

Complete uniform sets, neckties, pins, patches, and official lanyards are available at the Campus Bookstore and Admissions Office at all branches.

Student Welfare & Protection

Campus Safety & Health Advisories

At Datamex College of St. Adeline, student security, well-being, and health are proactive institutional commitments.

S.Y. 2026–2027 Committee

Anti-Bullying & Child Protection

"One Datamex, One Community, Zero Bullying." A safe, respectful, and inclusive space where every learner is valued.

  • Zero-Tolerance Policy: Strict prevention against harassment and cyberbullying.
  • Guidance Support: On-site Registered Guidance Counselor and Child Protection Officers.
  • Peer Advocates: Active student council and parent committee representation.
Clinic Guidelines

When Should You Stay Home?

"Your health matters. Let's stop the spread and keep our learning spaces safe and healthy."

  • Attendance Benchmarks: Clear clinic guidelines for fever (>38°C) or contagious symptoms.
  • Campus Clinic Nurse: Dedicated medical personnel ready for first aid and health monitoring.
  • Parent Coordination: Immediate notification to family members for recovery at home.
Campus Security

Active Threat Safety Drill

"Stay Informed. Be Prepared. Protect Yourself and Others." Institutional crisis response training.

  • RUN • HIDE • FIGHT: Standardized institutional emergency drills practiced across campus.
  • Police Coordination: Direct hotline to local PNP Valenzuela (8352 4000) & Unified 911.
  • Safety Marshals: Trained faculty marshals leading students safely during emergencies.
Air Quality Alert

Haze Alert & Air Quality Protocol

"Stay Informed. Take Caution. Protect Your Health. Safety First!" Smog and air advisory steps.

  • 4 Core Steps: Stay indoors, wear N95 face masks, seal drafts, safe commuting.
  • Respiratory Care: Dedicated support for students with asthma or sensitivities.
  • Ventilated Spaces: Controlled airflow throughout all computer labs and lecture halls.
