Admission Requirements

Prepare the following standard credentials for submission upon walk-in enrollment at your chosen branch.

Senior High School Applicants

Incoming Grade 11 & Grade 12 Transferees (100% Free Tuition with Voucher):

  • Original Form 138 (Grade 10 Report Card)
  • PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy with original for verification)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character from previous school
  • DepEd Voucher Certificate / ESC Certificate (if private school completer)
  • 4 pcs 2x2 ID Pictures with white background and name tag
  • Barangay Clearance of the student or parent

College & Technical Diploma Applicants

For BSIT, BSHM, and ACT Freshmen & Transferees:

  • Original Form 138 (Senior High School Report Card) or Form 137
  • PSA Authenticated Birth Certificate
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • 4 pcs 2x2 Recent Color ID Pictures
  • Transcript of Records (TOR) & Honorable Dismissal (for transferees)
  • Marriage Certificate (for married female applicants)
Simple & Hassle-Free

4-Step Walk-In Enrollment Process

Complete your registration smoothly in four convenient steps at our Admissions Office.

1

Inquire & Consult

Visit any DCSA campus branch. Meet our friendly admissions counselors to select your strand or degree program.

2

Submit Credentials

Present your Form 138 report card, PSA birth certificate, Good Moral certificate, and ID pictures.

3

Evaluation

Our Registrar will review your academic qualifications and certify your 100% DepEd Voucher subsidy.

4

Sectioning & ID

Receive your official registration form, class schedule, section assignment, and campus student ID!
